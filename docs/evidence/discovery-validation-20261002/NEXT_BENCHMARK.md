# Future controlled benchmark (not executed)

Hypothesis: Dream increases the probability of discovering a valuable, non-obvious
problem missed by strong conventional prompting frequently enough to justify its
additional cost and latency. This mission is a behavior/integrity regression check,
not that benchmark and not another full product dogfood cycle.

Conditions:
A one strong conventional prompt; B repeated strong conventional prompts using
approximately Dream-equivalent budget; C Dream discovery; D Create; E Dream to Create.
Pre-register prompts, budgets, domains and evaluation rubric before generation.
Use multiple operational domains (developer platforms, municipal maintenance, museum
operations, supply-chain quality, research tooling, accessibility coordination).
Within each domain keep subject, model family and evidence availability identical
where relevant. Explicitly separate discovery-only evidence withholding from later
review; include a labeled anchoring ablation with and without evidence in generation.

Allocate repeated independent trials and randomized order, record actual tokens,
cached tokens, monetary charges/unknown costs, calls, latency, failures and repairs.
Match B's actual budget to C within a declared tolerance; charge E both stages.
Do not hide partial runs or extra repair calls. Keep conventional prompts strong and
reviewed, not straw-man baselines. Record prior operator ideas before generation.

Blind candidate origin/order/provider to multiple independent domain reviewers.
Score distinct useful opportunity families, non-obviousness relative to recorded
priors, implementation usefulness, objective fit, feasibility, unsupported assumptions,
evidence awareness and provenance honesty. Evaluate problem discovery separately
from quality of solution prose. Retain all raw outputs and duplicate lineage;
use human cluster adjudication alongside deterministic proxies, never proxy counts
as proof of semantic novelty. Preserve highest novelty/usefulness/feasibility/fit and
balanced finalists rather than treating one weighted winner as the entire result.

For a preregistered bounded subset, ask implementers blinded to origin to assess
build usefulness against a common scope; no automatic full product builds. Empirical
claims require source-type provenance and declared validation maturity. Synthetic
controls and self-consistency do not establish external utility. Measure incremental
useful-family probability and yield per token/dollar/minute with uncertainty intervals,
inter-rater agreement and sensitivity to clustering/ranking. Report all domains,
including failures and negative results. Choose sample size after pilot variance;
do not claim superiority from this mission's one subject comparison.

Decision: justify added Dream/E cost only with a reproducible increase in useful,
non-obvious discoveries under comparable budgets. No execution or release authority
is assigned to candidate scores. Independent human review required for conclusion.
